Deine Einstellungen (Sprache, Stadt, Filter wie Allergien und Ernährungsweise, ein auf der Karte gewählter Punkt) werden nur lokal auf deinem Gerät gespeichert, damit sie beim nächsten Start erhalten bleiben (§ 25 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 TDDDG). Sie verlassen dein Gerät nicht. Beim Löschen der App werden sie entfernt. Hinweis: Angaben zu Allergien können Gesundheitsdaten sein. Sie bleiben ausschließlich auf deinem Gerät.

Rechtsgrundlage ist unser berechtigtes Interesse an einer funktionierenden Kartendarstellung (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO).

Absturzberichte und Nutzungsstatistiken erhalten wir nur, wenn du der Weitergabe an App-Entwickler in den Einstellungen deines Geräts zugestimmt hast. Sie werden uns anonymisiert von Apple bzw. Google bereitgestellt.

Privacy Policy

As of 3 November 2026 · applies to the “Beetroute” app (iOS and Android)

2. In short

  • No account, no sign-up.
  • No tracking, no analytics, no advertising.
  • We do not receive any personal data from you. The app has no server of its own that data is sent to.

3. Location

Only if you choose “My location” and allow access, the app reads your device’s location. It is used on your device only to calculate distances, recommend nearby restaurants and show your position on the map. It is not stored and not sent to us. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can revoke access at any time in your device settings.

4. Storage on your device

Your settings (language, city, filters such as allergies and diet, a point picked on the map) are stored locally on your device only, so they are kept for the next start. They never leave your device and are removed when you delete the app.

5. Map display (OpenFreeMap)

To show the map, the app loads map tiles and fonts from OpenFreeMap (openfreemap.org) and the map libraries Leaflet and MapLibre from the unpkg.com content delivery network. For this, your device technically has to send its IP address and the requested map area to these providers. We do not send your location itself. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a working map (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

6. Links to other services

When you open a restaurant’s website, a menu source or a maps app (Apple Maps, Google Maps, OpenStreetMap), you leave Beetroute and that provider’s privacy policy applies.

7. App stores and crash reports

The app is distributed via the Apple App Store and Google Play, which process data under their own responsibility. We only receive anonymised crash reports and usage statistics if you have agreed to share them with app developers in your device settings.

8. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.
